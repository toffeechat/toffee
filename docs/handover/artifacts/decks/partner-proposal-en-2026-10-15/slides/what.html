What Toffee is

A monthly subscription that feels like a private chat with the artist

1

Fans subscribe

A fan picks an artist and pays a monthly fee through the App Store or Google Play.

2

The artist sends

Text, photos, voice notes and short videos, whenever they like. Every fan receives it in a 1:1-style chat room.

3

Fans reply

Fans answer with text and emoji. The artist sees every reply; other fans never do.

Toffee · Partner proposal · 2

Reference point for Korean-aware partners: this is the same category as Bubble or Weverse DM in Korea. Emphasise that the fan does not see other fans, so it feels personal, while the artist only writes one message.
